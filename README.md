# anime-battles
Daily fictional anime fights log.
Day 1: Pain vs. Might Guy (Part 1)

The sky turned dark as Pain floated above the Hidden Leaf Village. Guy instantly opened the 6th Gate of View, speed-blitzing towards him with fiery punches. Pain unleashed "Shinra Tensei," blasting Guy backwards before he could land the killer blow!
Day 2: Pain vs. Might Guy (Part 2)

Guy activated the 7th Gate of Wonder, unleashing the devastating "Hirudora" tiger air pressure attack. Pain managed to block the shockwave using "Almighty Push," but the sheer force shattered the surrounding terrain. Both fighters stood bruised, preparing for their final ultimate techniques.
Day 3: Jiraiya vs. Itachi (Part 1)

Jiraiya summoned Gamabunta and trapped the area inside the Toad Mouth Dwelling to restrict movement. Itachi remained calm, activating his Mangekyou Sharingan and unleashing "Amaterasu" black flames to burn straight through the toad wall. As Itachi escaped the trap, Jiraiya began gathering nature energy to enter Sage Mode.
